Quantum Cosmology

Loop quantum cosmology, quantum gravity, and resolving singularities through quantum mechanics

This section is dedicated to the ongoing research projects of our group related to loop quantum cosmology. Initials of the faculty involved are in parentheses.

Numerical Solutions in Loop Quantum Cosmology

PS, GK

Loop quantum cosmology (LQC) is a symmetry-reduced application of loop quantum gravity (LQG), a theory that leads to a discrete structure of spacetime at a fundamental level. One main result of LQC is the resolution of cosmological and black hole singularities.

LQC ultimately leads to models of the Universe that exhibit a "big-bounce" as opposed to a "big-bang". This arises from the quantum nature of gravitation, which appears to be repulsive in the very early Universe.

Homogeneous Loop Quantum Cosmology

PS, GK

While homogeneous and isotropic cosmologies have been extensively studied in LQC, this project focuses on anisotropic models—Bianchi cosmologies and their solutions in LQC. These models are crucial for understanding the very early Universe.

An interesting recent discovery is that evolution through a singularity provides evidence of different physics on the other side, potentially indicating parameter change. This has profound implications for our understanding of quantum gravity and the origin of the Universe.

Black Holes in Loop Quantum Gravity

PS, GK

Loop quantum gravity also resolves the physical singularity present in the interior of black holes. Details of this work have significant implications for various aspects of black hole physics, including information loss and evaporation.

Parameter Change & Cosmological Natural Selection

GK

Cosmological Natural Selection (CNS) is an idea proposed by Lee Smolin in his book The Life of the Cosmos. The thesis is that physical constants appear tuned to ensure black holes arise and proliferate.

Smolin speculates on a mechanism for change in physical constants in a "big-bounce" scenario. This project probes the validity of CNS in the context of loop quantum cosmology.
