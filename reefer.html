The Reefer Container Supercomputer

Rack front

Earlier configuration

Rack side

Earlier side view

The "Reefer" is the big brother of the PS3 Gravity Grid — a large-scale heterogeneous computing cluster housed in a refrigerated shipping container ("reefer"), featuring PlayStation 3 consoles alongside IBM QS22 blade servers with Cell processors.

This system was built with support from the US Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL) under a CRADA agreement, and represents one of the earliest examples of a containerized high-performance computing facility. The reefer container provides both the physical housing and the cooling infrastructure.

Key Features

  • Refrigerated shipping container housing
  • Mix of PlayStation 3 consoles and IBM QS22 blades
  • Cell Broadband Engine processors throughout
  • Supported by AFRL CRADA agreement (10-RI-CRADA-09)
  • Used for gravitational wave and scientific computing research
