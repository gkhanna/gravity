Binary Black Hole Coalescence using Perturbation Theory

GK, SH, LB, RP, SF

This project deals with estimating properties of the gravitational waves produced by the merger of two black holes. This is of direct relevance to the various gravitational wave observatories being built world-wide (LIGO, LISA).

Radiative "Tails" in Black Hole Spacetimes

LB, GK, RP, SF

This project investigates the late-time behavior of various physical fields evolving in black hole spacetimes, known as radiative "tails". While these fields are known to exhibit power-law decay, the precise power-law index has been a matter of conflict in the literature — especially for rotating black holes.

We use highly accurate, long-term numerical evolutions to settle this matter. The log-log plot shows radiation from a black hole decaying over time, with a clear intermediate-time power-law decay and distinct late-time behavior.

Black Holes in Loop Quantum Gravity

GK, PS

Quantum gravity is expected to resolve the physical singularities (infinite density at the Big Bang and black hole centers) that plague classical general relativity. This project investigates this possibility in the context of loop quantum gravity.

Einstein@Home

GK

Einstein@Home uses your computer's idle time to search for spinning neutron stars (pulsars) using data from the LIGO and GEO gravitational wave detectors. We assist in optimizing this software for high-performance hardware such as GPUs and Cell BE architectures.
