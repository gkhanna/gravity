OpenCL for Scientific Computation

This section is dedicated to the ongoing research projects of our group related to the use of OpenCL for scientific computation. This work was supported under NSF grant PHY-1016906 and also by Apple, Nvidia and IBM. Initials of the faculty involved are in parentheses.

OpenCL (Open Computing Language) is a framework for writing programs that execute across heterogeneous platforms — CPUs, GPUs, and Cell processors. Our group was among the earliest to apply OpenCL to numerical relativity and gravitational wave computations.
