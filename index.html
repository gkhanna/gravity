Welcome to U²GRC

The UMass-URI Gravity Research Consortium is dedicated to theoretical and computational gravitational physics — from black hole dynamics and spacetime perturbation theory to quantum cosmology and high-performance scientific computing.

This site is a modern restoration of the original early-2000s gravity research pages, preserving the original research areas, people, and spirit while bringing the design into 2026.

Explore Research Meet the Team

Research Areas

Our work spans multiple domains of gravitational physics and computational science.

🌌

Black Holes

Perturbation theory, waveforms, ringdown, tails, and the dynamics of test particles in curved spacetime.

🌀

Quantum Cosmology

Loop quantum gravity, quantum geometry, and the quantum foundations of spacetime itself.

⚙️

Scientific Computation

High-performance computing, numerical methods, and the simulation of gravitational phenomena.

🎮

PS3 Cluster

A novel HPC platform built from PlayStation 3 consoles for parallel gravitational wave modeling.

📦

Reefer Container

Scaling beyond PS3s — a containerized HPC system for large-scale computational campaigns.

🔧

OpenCL & GPU

GPU-accelerated scientific computation using OpenCL and heterogeneous hardware architectures.

About U²GRC

📊

Research Focus

We investigate the strong-field regime of gravity through black hole physics, gravitational wave science, and quantum aspects of spacetime.

🖥️

Innovation in HPC

Pioneering novel computing platforms — from PlayStation 3 clusters to containerized HPC systems — for gravitational physics simulations.

🤝

Collaboration

A consortium spanning UMass Dartmouth and the University of Rhode Island, with collaborators at leading institutions worldwide.
